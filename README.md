# Hi, I'm Darko.

I am a **software architect and senior engineer building trustworthy AI systems**.

For 12+ years I have worked across product engineering, system architecture, cloud infrastructure, reliability, performance, and security. I like owning the whole path from an ambiguous product problem to a system a team can operate safely.

Right now I am applying that experience to agentic software: explicit identity, narrow tool contracts, retrieval and context, repeatable evaluation, human control, and safe failure modes. I am interested in useful automation, not impressive demos without a production contract.

I am open to remote **Software Architect**, **Senior/Staff Software Engineer**, and **AI Systems Engineer** roles with international product and platform teams.
